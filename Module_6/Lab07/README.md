### Assignment Overview

This assignment focuses on creating interactive 2D and 3D StoryMaps using **ArcGIS StoryMaps** and **ArcGIS Online 3D scenes**. You will build two separate StoryMaps:

- A **2D StoryMap** telling a story of your choice using maps, text, and images  
- A **3D StoryMap** showcasing part of the **TAMU campus** using 3D layers and web scenes  

These StoryMaps combine narrative storytelling with spatial visualization, allowing you to design an engaging, map-driven experience.

---

### Tasks

## **1. Build a 2D Story Map**

- **Decide the story you want to tell**  
- **Identify the data needed** to support your narrative  
- **Follow the instructions** provided in the lab to design your StoryMap  
- Include text, images, and at least one 2D map  

---

## **2. Build a 3D Web Scene Story Map**

- **Select a TAMU-focused theme** for your 3D story  
- **Use the TAMU 3D building layer** and other available campus layers  
- **Host the 3D scene in ArcGIS Online**  
- Integrate the 3D web scene into a StoryMap  
- Include narrative elements explaining your chosen campus topic
